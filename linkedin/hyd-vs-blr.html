A few observations 😅

Every city has its challenges. Some of these genuinely surprised me.

Housing

A decent 1–2 BHK at a fair price = winning a lottery. Brokerage of 20–45 days' rent.

🔥

Gas

No new connections, yet cylinders are easy in the black market?

🚌

Public transport

Finding a bus is an adventure. Rapido & Uber are the real default.

💧

Water

Maintenance + water bill + tanker charges, while high-rises keep rising.

💸

The jugaad economy

Same product, 2–3× the price depending on how much they think you can pay. MRP is a friendly suggestion.
